## 📋 小队简介  
### *About Us*

本小队创立于 **2025 年第 1 号台风蝴蝶 (Wutip)** 来临之际，由地球科学爱好者、摄影爱好者、气象爱好者共同创建，现已发展壮大。  
*Founded on the eve of Typhoon Wutip (2025), the first named storm of the season, this team was forged by Earth science devotees, photography enthusiasts, and weather fanatics—and has grown ever since.*

🎯 **方向 / Mission**  
以拍摄广东境内强对流、台风、锋面等激烈天气为主，不定期向 **@中国气象爱好者** 投稿，举办线下拍摄活动。  
*Focusing on documenting severe convection, typhoons, and frontal systems across Guangdong. We contribute to **@中国气象爱好者** and organize on-location shooting expeditions.*
